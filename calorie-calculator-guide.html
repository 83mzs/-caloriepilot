Calorie Calculator Guide

A calorie calculator provides an estimate of daily energy needs using common variables such as age, height, weight, sex and activity level.

What Does a Calorie Calculator Estimate?

It can estimate resting energy expenditure and, when activity is included, an approximate total daily energy expenditure.

Calorie Calculator vs BMR Calculator

A BMR Calculator focuses on estimated energy use at rest. A calorie calculator can use BMR together with an activity factor to estimate daily needs.

Calorie Calculator vs TDEE Calculator

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. The TDEE Calculator focuses specifically on estimating total daily expenditure.

Why Are Results Estimates?

Equations are based on population data and cannot measure every individual's metabolism. Activity, body composition and daily routine can change real energy needs.
