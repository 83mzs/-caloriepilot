How to Calculate a Calorie Deficit

A calorie deficit means consuming fewer calories than your body uses over time. The appropriate size varies by person, goal and circumstances.

Use TDEE as a starting estimate.

The basic calculation

Estimated calorie target = estimated maintenance calories − planned calorie deficit. For example, 2,200 estimated maintenance calories minus 500 calories gives a 1,700 calorie target.

Why results can differ

Mathematical estimates cannot capture every factor affecting energy expenditure. Activity, body composition, changes in body weight and normal variation can all affect real-world results.

FAQ

What is a good calorie deficit?

There is no single deficit suitable for everyone. A moderate approach is often more practical than an aggressive restriction, but individual needs differ.

Does a calorie deficit guarantee weight loss?

A sustained energy deficit generally promotes weight loss, but the rate and scale of change vary.

Are very low calorie diets safe?

Very low calorie diets can be inappropriate for some people. If you are under 18, pregnant, have a medical condition or have specific nutrition concerns, seek qualified professional guidance.
