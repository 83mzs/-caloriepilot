How to Calculate a Calorie Deficit

A calorie deficit means consuming fewer calories than you expend over time. The size of a deficit can vary by person and goal.

Basic calculation

Start with an estimate of maintenance calories, often approximated with TDEE. A simple deficit calculation is maintenance calories minus planned calorie intake.

Use the calculator

Try the Calorie Deficit Calculator to explore an estimate. The TDEE Calculator can help estimate maintenance calories first.

Remember

Calculated values are estimates. Actual energy needs and changes in body weight can differ from mathematical predictions.
