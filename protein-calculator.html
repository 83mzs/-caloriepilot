Why Is Protein Important?

Protein is an essential nutrient that helps support muscles, tissues and many important functions in the body.

How Much Protein Do You Need?

Daily protein requirements can vary depending on body weight, activity level, training goals and other individual factors. This calculator provides a general estimate based on body weight and activity.

Protein and Exercise

People who regularly perform resistance training or other forms of exercise may have higher protein requirements than people with low physical activity.

Protein for Muscle Building

Adequate protein intake can support muscle maintenance and recovery when combined with appropriate training and overall nutrition.

Are These Results Exact?

No. The result is an estimate and should be used as general informational guidance rather than a medical recommendation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is protein intake calculated?

This calculator estimates protein needs by multiplying body weight in kilograms by a protein factor associated with the selected activity level.

Should active people eat more protein?

Physical activity and training can increase protein requirements, particularly for people who regularly perform resistance training.

Can protein help build muscle?

Protein provides amino acids needed for muscle repair and maintenance. Muscle growth also depends on resistance training, overall nutrition and recovery.

Can I use this calculator every day?

You can use the calculator whenever you want to estimate your daily protein target based on your current weight and activity.
