Estimated Daily Maintenance Calories

What Is a Calorie Calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories your body may need each day based on factors such as age, body weight, height, sex and physical activity.

The result can help you understand your estimated maintenance calorie intake and make more informed nutrition and fitness decisions.

How Does the Calorie Calculator Work?

CaloriePilot uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). Your BMR is then multiplied by an activity factor to estimate Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

What Is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, is an estimate of the energy your body uses at rest to support essential functions such as breathing, circulation and maintaining body temperature.

What Is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total amount of energy you may use throughout a typical day, including physical activity.

Are These Results Exact?

No. Calorie calculations are estimates. Individual calorie needs can vary depending on body composition, activity, lifestyle and other factors.

The results provided by CaloriePilot are for general informational purposes and are not medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories do I need per day?

Your daily calorie needs depend on factors including age, weight, height, sex and activity level. Use the calculator above for an individual estimate.

What is a maintenance calorie level?

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the amount of energy needed to maintain your current body weight based on your typical activity.

Can I use a calorie calculator for weight loss?

A calorie calculator can provide an estimate of maintenance calories. People commonly use that estimate as a starting point when planning their nutrition, but individual needs can vary.
