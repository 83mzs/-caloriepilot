What Is a Calorie Calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories your body may need each day based on factors such as age, sex, height, weight and physical activity.

Your estimated daily calorie requirement can be useful when planning a weight-loss, weight-maintenance or weight-gain goal.

How Does the Calorie Calculator Work?

CaloriePilot estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Your estimated BMR is then multiplied by an activity factor to estimate your Total Daily Energy Expenditure.

What Is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, is an estimate of the energy your body uses to support essential functions while at rest.

What Is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It represents an estimate of the total calories you burn during a typical day, including physical activity.

Calorie Deficit for Weight Loss

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses. A commonly used starting point is a moderate deficit rather than an extremely restrictive diet.

Calorie Surplus for Weight Gain

A calorie surplus means consuming more energy than your body uses. A moderate surplus may be used when the goal is gradual weight gain.

Important Note

Calorie calculations are estimates and individual energy needs can vary. This calculator is intended for informational purposes and is not medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a calorie calculator?

Calorie calculators provide estimates rather than exact measurements. Your actual calorie needs can vary based on many individual factors.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Weight loss generally requires a sustained calorie deficit. The appropriate calorie target varies from person to person.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR estimates energy used at rest, while TDEE estimates total daily energy expenditure including activity.

Can I use this calculator every day?

You can use the calculator whenever you want to review your estimated calorie needs. Your results may change when your body measurements or activity level change.
