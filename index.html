Frequently Asked Questions

What is a calorie calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates your daily calorie needs using information such as age, sex, height, weight and activity level.

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the energy your body uses at rest.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates total daily calorie expenditure including activity.

Are calculator results exact?

No. Calculator results are estimates and individual needs can vary.
