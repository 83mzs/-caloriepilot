CaloriePilot Fitness Calculators

CaloriePilot provides simple tools for estimating calorie needs, metabolism, body mass index and nutrition targets.

BMR

Basal Metabolic Rate is an estimate of the energy your body uses at rest.

TDEE

Total Daily Energy Expenditure estimates the calories you may burn during a typical day including activity.

BMI

BMI is a simple weight-to-height screening measurement. It does not directly measure body fat or overall health.
