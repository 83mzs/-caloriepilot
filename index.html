About CaloriePilot

CaloriePilot is a free online fitness calculator platform designed to make calorie and nutrition calculations simple and easy to understand.

Our tools provide estimates for daily calories, metabolism, body mass index, macronutrients, protein requirements and calorie deficits.

How does a calorie calculator work?

A calorie calculator estimates daily energy needs using age, sex, height, weight and activity level.

What is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, estimates the energy your body uses at rest.

What is TDEE?

TDEE, or Total Daily Energy Expenditure, estimates total energy use during a typical day, including physical activity.

What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a screening measurement based on height and weight.

What is a calorie deficit?

A calorie deficit occurs when calorie intake is lower than energy expenditure over time.

CaloriePilot results are estimates based on commonly used formulas and should not be considered medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a calorie calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories you may need each day based on age, sex, height, weight and activity level.

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the calories your body uses at rest.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total calories you use each day including activity.

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a screening measurement calculated using height and weight.

What is a calorie deficit?

A calorie deficit means consuming fewer calories than your body uses over time.

Are CaloriePilot calculator results exact?

No. Results are estimates based on commonly used formulas. Individual calorie and nutrition needs can vary.
