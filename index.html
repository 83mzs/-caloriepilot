Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the calories your body uses at rest to support essential functions.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the calories you may burn during a normal day including physical activity.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Weight loss generally requires a calorie deficit. The appropriate calorie target varies between individuals.

What is BMI?

BMI is a simple weight-to-height screening measurement. It does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

Are CaloriePilot results accurate?

The calculators provide estimates using commonly used formulas. Actual energy needs can vary from person to person.

How does a calorie calculator work?

A calorie calculator typically uses information such as age, sex, height, weight and physical activity to estimate daily energy requirements.

What is Basal Metabolic Rate?

Basal Metabolic Rate, or BMR, is an estimate of the amount of energy your body uses while at rest to maintain essential physiological functions.

What is Total Daily Energy Expenditure?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure, or TDEE, estimates the total amount of energy you may use throughout a normal day, including physical activity.

Why calculate macros?

Macronutrients include protein, carbohydrates and fat. A macro calculator can provide an estimated starting point for organizing daily nutrition.
