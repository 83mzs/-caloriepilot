About CaloriePilot

CaloriePilot is a free online fitness calculator platform designed to make calorie and nutrition calculations simple and easy to understand.

Our calculators use commonly used formulas to provide estimates for daily calories, metabolism and nutrition.

How does a calorie calculator work?

A calorie calculator estimates your daily energy needs using information such as age, sex, height, weight and activity level.

Why calculate BMR and TDEE?

BMR estimates the energy your body uses at rest, while TDEE estimates your total daily energy expenditure after considering activity.

Results are estimates and should not be considered medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a calorie calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories you may need each day based on factors such as age, sex, height, weight and activity level.

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the calories your body uses at rest to support basic functions.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total calories you burn each day including physical activity.

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a screening measurement calculated using height and weight.

How accurate are calorie calculator results?

Results are estimates based on commonly used formulas. Individual calorie and nutrition requirements can vary.
