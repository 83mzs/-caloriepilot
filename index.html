Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the calories your body uses at rest to support essential functions.

What is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the calories you may burn during a normal day including physical activity.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Weight loss generally requires consuming fewer calories than your body uses. The appropriate target varies from person to person.

What is BMI?

BMI is a simple weight-to-height screening measurement. It does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

How calorie calculations work

Calorie estimates generally use information such as age, sex, height, weight and physical activity. The results are estimates because real energy expenditure varies between individuals.

Understanding BMR

BMR represents estimated energy expenditure at rest. CaloriePilot uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for its BMR estimate.

Understanding TDEE

TDEE estimates daily energy expenditure after accounting for physical activity. It is commonly used as a starting point for calorie planning.
