What Is a Calorie Calculator?

A calorie calculator estimates how many calories your body may need each day based on factors such as age, sex, height, weight and physical activity.

Your estimated daily calorie requirement can help when planning a weight-loss, weight-maintenance or weight-gain goal.

How Does This Calculator Work?

CaloriePilot estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Your estimated BMR is then multiplied by an activity factor to estimate Total Daily Energy Expenditure.

What Is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the energy your body uses to support essential functions while at rest.

What Is TDEE?

TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure. It estimates the total calories you may burn during a typical day, including physical activity.

Calorie Deficit

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses. A moderate deficit is generally preferable to extremely restrictive eating.

Calorie Surplus

A calorie surplus means consuming more energy than your body uses. It may be used when the goal is gradual weight gain.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a calorie calculator?

Calorie calculators provide estimates rather than exact measurements. Individual calorie needs can vary.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Weight loss generally requires a sustained calorie deficit. The appropriate target varies by person.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR estimates energy used at rest, while TDEE estimates total daily energy expenditure including activity.
