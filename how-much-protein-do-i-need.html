How Much Protein Do I Need?

Protein needs can vary with body size, activity, training and individual goals. A calculator can provide a useful starting estimate rather than an exact requirement.

Using body weight

Many nutrition approaches estimate protein from body weight. The appropriate range depends on the person and context.
