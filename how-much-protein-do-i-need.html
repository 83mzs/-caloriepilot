How Much Protein Do I Need?

Protein needs vary with body size, activity, training, goals and individual circumstances. A calculator can provide a useful starting estimate rather than an exact requirement.

Protein and body weight

Many nutrition approaches estimate daily protein using body weight. The useful range depends on the person's activity and goals.

Protein and exercise

People who regularly train, particularly with resistance exercise, may need more protein than people with low activity. Protein works together with adequate calories, training and recovery.

Protein for muscle maintenance

Adequate protein supplies amino acids used for tissue maintenance and repair. Total diet quality and sufficient energy also matter.

How to estimate your target

Use the Protein Calculator to generate a general estimate based on weight and activity. Reassess when your weight or training routine changes.

FAQ

How is protein intake calculated?

This calculator multiplies body weight in kilograms by a factor associated with the selected activity level.

Should active people eat more protein?

Training can increase protein needs, especially with regular resistance exercise.

Can protein alone build muscle?

No. Muscle growth also depends on resistance training, adequate energy, recovery and overall nutrition.

Do medical conditions affect protein needs?

Yes. Certain medical conditions can require individualized nutrition guidance. Consult a qualified health professional when appropriate.
