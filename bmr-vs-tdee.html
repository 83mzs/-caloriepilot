BMR vs TDEE: What's the Difference?

BMR and TDEE are related but answer different questions. BMR focuses on energy used at rest, while TDEE estimates total daily energy expenditure.

BMR
Resting energy estimate.
TDEE
Total daily energy estimate including activity.

What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate estimates the energy your body uses at rest to support essential functions such as breathing and circulation.

What is TDEE?

Total Daily Energy Expenditure estimates energy used across a typical day, including activity and movement. It is generally higher than BMR.

Which should I use?

Use the BMR Calculator when you want a resting estimate. Use the TDEE Calculator when you want an estimate that includes activity.

How are they connected?

A common approach is to estimate BMR first and then apply an activity factor to estimate TDEE. This makes BMR a useful building block for TDEE.

FAQ

Is TDEE the same as maintenance calories?

TDEE can be used as a starting estimate for maintenance calories, but actual maintenance needs vary.

Can BMR be higher than TDEE?

In normal calculations TDEE includes activity on top of resting needs, so it should generally be higher than BMR.

Are BMR and TDEE exact?

No. Both are estimates and individual energy expenditure can differ.
