BMR vs TDEE: What's the Difference?

What is BMR?

BMR, or Basal Metabolic Rate, estimates the energy your body needs at rest to support basic physiological functions.

What is TDEE?

TDEE, or Total Daily Energy Expenditure, estimates total daily energy use after accounting for activity. It is generally higher than BMR.

Which should I use?

Use the BMR Calculator to understand your resting estimate and the TDEE Calculator when you want an estimate that includes activity.

Both are estimates, not exact measurements. Individual energy needs can vary.
