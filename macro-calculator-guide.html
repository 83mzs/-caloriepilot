Macro Calculator Guide: Protein, Carbs and Fat

Macros are the three major macronutrients: protein, carbohydrates and fat. A macro plan distributes total calorie intake between them.

Protein

Protein provides amino acids needed for tissue maintenance and repair. Protein targets can vary with body weight, activity and goals.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates provide 4 calories per gram and are an important energy source. Food choices also contribute fiber and micronutrients.

Fat

Fat provides 9 calories per gram and has important structural and physiological roles.

How macros connect to calories

Because each macronutrient contributes a different amount of energy per gram, macro targets can be converted into calories. The total macro calories should align with the overall calorie target.

FAQ

Do I need to hit macros exactly?

Not necessarily. Macro targets are estimates and practical flexibility can make a nutrition plan easier to maintain.

Which macro is most important?

The appropriate balance depends on the person and goal. Adequate protein and overall nutrition are important, while carbohydrate and fat needs vary.

Are macro calculator results exact?

No. They are starting estimates and individual nutrition needs vary.
