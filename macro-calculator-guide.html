Macro Calculator Guide

Macros are the three major macronutrients: protein, carbohydrates and fat. A macro plan distributes calories between them.

Protein

Protein contributes 4 calories per gram and is important for maintaining and building body tissues.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates provide 4 calories per gram and are a major energy source.

Fat

Fat provides 9 calories per gram and plays important structural and physiological roles.
