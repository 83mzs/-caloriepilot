About CaloriePilot

CaloriePilot provides free browser-based tools that estimate calorie needs, BMR, TDEE, BMI, calorie deficits, macronutrients and protein intake.

How our calculators work

Where applicable, energy calculations use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation together with standard activity factors. Results are estimates and can differ from measured needs.

Editorial approach

We explain each calculator’s purpose, formula and limitations in plain language. Content is reviewed when calculation logic or guidance changes.

Medical notice
