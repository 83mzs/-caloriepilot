What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used measurement that compares a person's weight with their height.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

The formula is: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m)

BMI Categories

For adults, BMI values are commonly grouped into categories such as underweight, normal range, overweight and obesity range.

BMI Limitations

BMI is a general screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat. It may not accurately represent body composition for every person, including people with high muscle mass.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal BMI?

For many adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the normal or healthy range.

Is BMI the same for men and women?

The standard adult BMI calculation uses the same formula for men and women. Interpretation can depend on age and individual factors.

Can BMI tell me how much body fat I have?

No. BMI does not directly measure body fat percentage or body composition.

Is BMI suitable for children?

Adult BMI categories should not be applied directly to children. Children and teenagers are generally assessed using age- and sex-specific growth charts.
