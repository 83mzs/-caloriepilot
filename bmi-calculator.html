What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measurement that compares a person's weight with their height.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m)

BMI Categories

For adults, BMI values are commonly grouped into underweight, normal range, overweight and obesity range. These categories are general screening ranges rather than a complete assessment of health.

BMI Limitations

BMI does not directly measure body fat or body composition. It may be less representative for some people, including those with high muscle mass.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal BMI?

For many adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is commonly classified as the normal or healthy range.

Is BMI the same for men and women?

The standard adult BMI formula is the same for men and women, while individual interpretation can depend on age and other factors.

Can BMI tell me my body fat percentage?

No. BMI is not a direct measurement of body fat percentage.

Is adult BMI suitable for children?

Adult BMI categories should not be applied directly to children. Children and teenagers are generally assessed using age- and sex-specific growth charts.
