What Is a Calorie Deficit?

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses over time. A sustained calorie deficit can contribute to weight loss.

How Does the Calculator Work?

The calculator subtracts your selected calorie deficit from your estimated maintenance calories.

For example, if your estimated maintenance level is 2,200 calories and you choose a 500 calorie deficit, the estimated target would be 1,700 calories per day.

Common Calorie Deficit Levels

  • 250 calories per day - smaller deficit
  • 500 calories per day - moderate deficit
  • 750 calories per day - larger deficit

Why Is the Result Only an Estimate?

Daily calorie needs can vary because of factors such as activity, body composition, age and changes in body weight. Therefore, calculator results should be treated as estimates rather than exact requirements.

Important Safety Information

A calorie target should be appropriate for your individual needs. Very low calorie diets may be inappropriate for some people. If you have medical conditions, are pregnant, are under 18, or have specific nutritional concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant dietary changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good calorie deficit?

There is no single deficit that is appropriate for everyone. A moderate deficit is commonly used, but individual needs vary.

Does a calorie deficit cause weight loss?

A sustained energy deficit generally leads to weight loss over time, although actual results can vary between individuals.

Should I use my maintenance calories?

The calculator works best when you enter a reasonable estimate of your current maintenance calorie needs.

Can I lose weight without counting calories?

Yes. Some people manage their weight without formally counting calories by adjusting food portions, food choices and activity.

Are these calorie targets medical advice?

No. CaloriePilot provides general informational estimates only. The calculator is not a substitute for professional medical or nutritional advice.
