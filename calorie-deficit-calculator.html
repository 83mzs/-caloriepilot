What Is a Calorie Deficit?

A calorie deficit occurs when you consume fewer calories than your body uses over time. A sustained energy deficit can contribute to weight loss.

How Does the Calculator Work?

The calculator subtracts the selected calorie deficit from estimated maintenance calories. For example, 2,200 maintenance calories minus a 500 calorie deficit gives an estimated target of 1,700 calories per day.

Common Calorie Deficit Levels

  • 250 calories per day - smaller deficit
  • 500 calories per day - moderate deficit
  • 750 calories per day - larger deficit

Why Is the Result Only an Estimate?

Daily calorie needs can vary because of activity, body composition, age and changes in body weight. Results should be treated as estimates rather than exact requirements.

Important Safety Information

A calorie target should be appropriate for your individual needs. Very low calorie diets may be inappropriate for some people. If you have medical conditions, are pregnant, are under 18, or have specific nutritional concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional before making significant dietary changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good calorie deficit?

There is no single deficit appropriate for everyone. A moderate deficit is commonly used, but individual needs vary.

Does a calorie deficit cause weight loss?

A sustained energy deficit generally leads to weight loss over time, although actual results vary.

Should I use my maintenance calories?

The calculator works best when you enter a reasonable estimate of your current maintenance calorie needs.

Can I lose weight without counting calories?

Yes. Some people manage weight without formally counting calories by adjusting portions, food choices and activity.

Are these calorie targets medical advice?

No. CaloriePilot provides general informational estimates only and is not a substitute for professional medical or nutritional advice.
