What Are Macronutrients?

Macronutrients, commonly called macros, are protein, carbohydrates and dietary fat. They provide energy and support important functions in the body.

How Does This Macro Calculator Work?

The calculator subtracts calories from the selected protein and fat amounts from your daily calorie target. The remaining calories are converted into an estimated carbohydrate amount.

Protein provides approximately 4 calories per gram, carbohydrates approximately 4 calories per gram, and fat approximately 9 calories per gram.

Protein

Protein provides amino acids that support muscle maintenance, tissue repair and other functions.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are an important source of energy and are found in foods such as grains, fruits, vegetables and legumes.

Fat

Dietary fat provides energy and supports functions including absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.
