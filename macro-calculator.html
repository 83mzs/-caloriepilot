What Are Macronutrients?

Macronutrients, commonly called macros, are nutrients that provide energy and support important functions in the body. The three main macronutrients are protein, carbohydrates and fat.

Protein

Protein provides amino acids that support muscle maintenance, tissue repair and many other functions in the body.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are an important source of energy. They are found in foods such as grains, fruits, vegetables and legumes.

Fat

Dietary fat provides energy and supports several important functions, including the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins.

How Does This Macro Calculator Work?
