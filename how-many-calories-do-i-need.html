How Many Calories Do I Need a Day?

Your daily calorie needs depend on factors such as age, sex, height, weight and physical activity. There is no single calorie number that fits everyone.

Maintenance calories

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the energy your body uses over a typical day. A calorie calculator commonly estimates this using BMR and an activity level.

How to estimate your needs

Start with the Calorie Calculator for a practical estimate. You can also compare your result with the TDEE Calculator.

For weight goals

People may adjust calorie intake above or below estimated maintenance depending on their goal. Real-world needs vary, so trends in body weight and energy should be considered over time.

Note: Calculator results are estimates and are not medical advice.
