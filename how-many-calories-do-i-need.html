How Many Calories Do I Need?

Daily calorie needs vary with age, body weight, height, sex, activity and lifestyle. A calculator provides an estimate that can be used as a starting point.

Start with BMR

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) estimates energy used at rest to support essential functions. It is not the same as the number of calories you need for an entire day.

Then estimate TDEE

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) adds an activity estimate to resting energy needs. It is commonly used as a starting estimate for maintenance calories.

Maintenance calories

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the intake that may maintain body weight under typical conditions. Actual needs can differ, so changes over time are useful feedback.

Calories for different goals

People may eat around maintenance for weight stability, use an appropriate deficit for weight loss, or an appropriate surplus for weight gain. The right target depends on the individual.

FAQ

How many calories should I eat?

There is no universal number. Use the Calorie Calculator with your measurements and activity level to get an estimate.

Do calorie needs change?

Yes. Changes in weight, activity, age and routine can change estimated energy requirements.

Are calculator results exact?

No. They are estimates rather than direct measurements of energy expenditure.
