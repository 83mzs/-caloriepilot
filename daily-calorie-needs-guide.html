Daily Calorie Needs: What Affects Your Calories?

Your daily energy needs are influenced by several factors. A calorie calculator combines common inputs to provide a useful starting estimate.

Age and Calorie Needs

Age is one factor used by common calorie equations. Energy needs can change over a lifetime as body size, activity and body composition change.

Height and Weight

Height and body weight are important inputs in equations used to estimate resting energy expenditure.

Sex

Many established calorie equations use sex as an input because average energy expenditure differs between populations.

Activity Level

Daily movement and exercise can substantially affect total energy expenditure. This is why activity level is included in TDEE and calorie estimates.
