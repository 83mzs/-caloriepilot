Daily Calorie Needs: What Affects Your Calories?

Your daily energy needs are influenced by several factors. Calorie equations combine common inputs to provide a useful starting estimate, but real-world needs can vary from person to person.

Quick start: use the Calorie Calculator to estimate daily needs, then explore BMR and TDEE for more context.

Age and Calorie Needs

Age is one factor used by common calorie equations. Energy requirements can change over a lifetime as body size, activity and body composition change.

Height and Weight

Height and body weight are important inputs in equations used to estimate resting energy expenditure. Changes in body weight can also change estimated daily requirements.

Sex

Many established calorie equations use sex as an input because average energy expenditure differs between populations. This is a statistical input, not a complete description of an individual's metabolism.

Activity Level

Exercise and everyday movement can substantially affect total energy expenditure. This is why activity level is included in TDEE and daily calorie estimates.

What Are Maintenance Calories?

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the energy intake that would maintain body weight under typical conditions. They are not a fixed number and can change with activity and body weight.

How BMR and TDEE Fit Together

BMR estimates energy used at rest. TDEE extends the estimate by accounting for activity and daily movement.

Why Your Actual Needs May Differ

Calorie formulas are estimates based on population-level relationships. Individual energy expenditure can differ because of body composition, routine, exercise, sleep and other factors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories do I need each day?

Your calorie needs depend on your personal characteristics and activity. Use the CaloriePilot calculator for an individualized estimate.

Do active people need more calories?

In general, more physical activity increases total energy expenditure, although the amount varies between people.

Do calorie needs change after losing weight?

They can. Changes in body weight and activity can change estimated energy requirements.

Is BMR the same as daily calorie needs?

No. BMR is an estimate of resting energy use, while daily calorie needs generally include activity and movement.
