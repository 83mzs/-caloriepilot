What Is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates the amount of energy your body uses while at rest to support essential functions such as breathing, circulation and maintaining body temperature.

How Is BMR Calculated?

CaloriePilot uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate BMR using age, body weight, height and sex.

Why Is BMR Important?

An estimated BMR can help you understand basic energy requirements and provides a starting point when estimating total daily energy expenditure.

BMR vs TDEE

BMR estimates energy used at rest. TDEE, or Total Daily Energy Expenditure, includes physical activity and daily movement.

Are BMR Results Exact?

No. BMR calculations are estimates. Individual energy requirements can vary with body composition, activity, lifestyle and other factors.

CaloriePilot provides general informational estimates and does not provide medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does BMR mean?

BMR means Basal Metabolic Rate. It estimates energy used at rest to maintain essential functions.

Does BMR change with age?

BMR can change over time as factors such as body weight, body composition and age change.

Is BMR the same as maintenance calories?

No. BMR represents energy used at rest, while maintenance calories generally include daily activity and movement.

Can BMR be used to estimate TDEE?

Yes. A BMR estimate can be combined with an activity factor to estimate TDEE.
